# Logan's taste rubric (how the judge should pick)

This is the distilled "how Logan picks" that the judge reads on every run. It starts
mostly empty and grows from `taste-log.jsonl` corrections. The more it grows, the less
Logan has to correct, and the more it can pick for other people the way Logan would.

## Locked (do not relearn)
- Framing: full-frame 9:16 center crop, zoom 1.0, whole body in frame, no punch-in.
- Fit-to-vibe beats raw similarity: the clip that serves the beat's feeling wins over the
  clip that merely matches the movement.
- Variety: no reusing the same clip across beats; vary locations across the reel.

## Learned preferences (grows from corrections)
Judge the MOMENT, not the shot category. Mirror, facing-camera, wide, close - any of them can
be great or bad depending on the moment. Never ban a whole category; keep variety across shot
types. The tests that actually matter:
- **Clearly the subject.** Logan should read as the clear subject of the shot. A mirror shot is
  good when it is obviously him and intentional; it only fails when you cannot tell he is the
  subject (you only notice him as a reflection). (grace_judge_v1 B4, Bible-gym_judge_v1 B6)
- **Well-framed at the chosen moment.** In frame at the in-point, not drifting out or flickering
  in and out. A deliberate move through frame is fine; an accidental half-out one is not.
  (grace_judge_v1 B5, Bible-gym_judge_v1 B5)
- **Training, not performing or setting up.** Real training reps of ANY shot type are good,
  including facing the camera. What fails is a moment where he stares into the camera or is
  getting ready for the shot / doing a skit bit. (grace_judge_v1 B7, Bible-gym_judge_v1 B8)
- **Fully in frame** and **candid-not-setup** (from DISCIPLINE) still hold, as moment-level
  tests, not category bans.
- **Technical quality.** The footage itself must be clean - no grain, noise, softness, or
  camera glitches. A perfect shot choice is still a reject if the clip looks low-quality.
  (grace_judge_v3 B4: right shot, but grainy - cut it) Detectable via a noise/sharpness score.

## Known traps (where the judge has been wrong)
- Over-generalizing one bad clip into a category ban ("mirror = bad", "facing camera = bad").
  Do not. Re-pick a better MOMENT from the full variety of shot types.
- clip 116 (Back/C1499) is a specific clip where Logan is not clearly the subject - skip that
  one clip, not mirror shots in general.
